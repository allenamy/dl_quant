## Empirical seed variance on y_1800

Even with `cudnn.deterministic=True` (commit 7efb6cc) the 4-seed measured stdev is:

- 3-fold pooled best Pearson: **±0.0055** (mean 0.033)
- 3-fold pooled best Spearman: **±0.005** (mean 0.039)
- 3-fold pooled EMA Pearson: **±0.0091** (mean 0.029)
- 3-fold pooled EMA Spearman: **±0.0092** (mean 0.038)

EMA variance is **~30% of metric magnitude** — exactly matching the rough estimate from earlier mid-train val_corr observations. **Single-seed conclusions on y_1800 are unreliable; multi-seed is mandatory.** This empirically nails down anti-pattern #14.
