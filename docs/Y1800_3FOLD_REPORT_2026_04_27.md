## Findings

1. **Best Pearson (best_model.pt):** `phase3c_val90_embargo30` at **+0.025**, beats ema_pool by +0.003 P and equals on Spearman.
2. **Best Spearman (overall):** `phase3c_val90_embargo30` EMA at **+0.037**, beats ema_pool best (+0.033) and EMA (+0.027). Confirms split engineering (val=90 + embargo=30) is the **only intervention** to materially lift S so far.
3. **GRU is dead** — both best and EMA lag every other config; variance compression to ~0.03. Reject GRU backbone for y_1800.
4. **Mamba ≈ baseline** — 3-fold EMA P=0.019 / S=0.014. No Mamba advantage over `ema_pool` or `phase3c`. Reject Mamba on y_1800.
5. **EMA usually helps** — closes the gap or wins outright on every config except `phase3c` Pearson (where best `best_model.pt` wins by +0.005). Selection: use whichever wins per config.
6. **A2 GLU killed** at fold 0 ep 18 (seed=42 EMA C=0.028 vs Phase 3c ≈0.040 at same epoch). GLU fusion does **not** add value on top of the split fix.

## Anti-pattern reinforcement

All capacity additions (Phase 1 σ-anchor, Phase 1 diff Spearman, CRPS, Phase 2 FiLM, Phase 4 multi-scale, Phase A1 Transformer, Phase A2 GLU) failed to beat the simple `phase3c` configuration in fold-0 screen or 3-fold confirmation. **Pattern: on y_1800 single-asset, capacity ≠ generalization. Only val/embargo split engineering and EMA averaging produce real lifts.**

This is consistent with CLAUDE.md anti-pattern #14 (single-fold unreliable) and the broader y_600 finding that single-asset architecture innovations stall at the same ~0.025–0.040 IC ceiling.
