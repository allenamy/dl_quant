## §4 Frozen analysis specification

**§4.1 Series and criterion.** Matrix `G[t, n] = g` of member n at anchor t (W_FULL rows). Criterion everywhere = annualized net Sharpe `SR = mean(g) / std(g, ddof=1) · √2190` on the rows in question.

**§4.2 CSCV-PBO** (AMENDMENT 5 (a); Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu). For each family × seed × window with T rows: drop the first `T mod 16` rows (oldest; W_FULL drops 6, W_ALPHA 2, FROZEN 0); 16 contiguous equal blocks in time order; all C(16,8) = 12,870 splits (IS = 8 blocks, OOS = the other 8). `n* = argmax SR_IS` (first index on exact ties, member_id order). `ω = rank(SR_OOS[n*]) / (N+1)` with ascending average ranks; `λ = ln(ω/(1−ω))`; **PBO = #{λ ≤ 0} / 12,870**. Also reported: OLS slope, intercept and correlation of `SR_OOS[n*]` on `SR_IS[n*]` over the 12,870 splits; **P(OOS loss) = #{SR_OOS[n*] < 0} / 12,870**; median λ; the five most frequently selected members. Splits overlap, so no CI is attached to PBO (frequency over all splits, as the method defines it).

**§4.3 DSR** (AMENDMENT 5 (b); Bailey & López de Prado 2014). Per-period (non-annualized) `SR̂_n` on the window; `V = var(SR̂_n, ddof=1)` over members; `N_eff = (Σλ_i)² / Σλ_i²` of the eigenvalues of the members' Pearson correlation matrix of g on the window; `SR0(N) = √V · [(1−γ)·Φ⁻¹(1−1/N) + γ·Φ⁻¹(1−1/(N·e))]`, γ = 0.5772156649 (N floored at 2 if below 2, flagged). For a target member k with T rows, skew `γ3 = skew(g_k, bias=False)`, kurtosis `γ4 = kurtosis(g_k, fisher=False, bias=False)`: `PSR(SR*) = Φ[(SR̂_k − SR*)·√(T−1) / √(1 − γ3·SR̂_k + (γ4−1)/4·SR̂_k²)]`.
Reported: **P(true SR > 0) = PSR(SR0(N))** (the DSR) and **P(true SR > 3.0) = PSR(3.0/√2190 + SR0(N))** — T6's operational extension: the selection benchmark shifted to a true annualized Sharpe of 3.0; also the undeflated references PSR(0) and PSR(3.0/√2190). N ∈ {**N_eff** (primary), **N_raw = number of members** (matrix count), **300** (AMENDMENT 5), **825** and **1,221** (N ledger §4.8, low / high)}. Targets: **SEL** = the member with the highest SR on the window (canonical DSR target) and **A0** (in-service form) with the same SR0.

**§4.4 Nested walk-forward selection** (AMENDMENT 5 (c)). W_FULL out-of-sample segments: 2023, 2024, 2025 (calendar years) and 2026-01-01 … 2026-08-30 20Z. For each segment, training rows = every W_FULL anchor before the segment start (expanding from 2022-01-31 00Z); `n* = argmax SR_train`; record n*, SR_train, SR on the segment, mean g on the segment. Nested series = concatenation of the selected members' g over the segments. **H\*** = full-window hindsight best (max SR over W_FULL). Reported: SR_nested; SR of H\* on the same span (2023-01-01 … end); SR of A0 on the span; span hindsight best (max SR on the span); **selection haircut (primary) = SR_nested − SR(H\*, span)**; level haircut = SR_nested − SR(H\*, W_FULL). FROZEN: segments 2025-03-01 … 2025-12-31 20Z and 2026-01-01 … 2026-08-10 20Z, training = all W_FULL anchors before each segment start; H\* = max SR on FROZEN; haircut = SR_nested − SR(H\*, FROZEN). CI95 of SR_nested and of the paired haircut (same anchors): UTC-day block bootstrap, 2,000 draws, `default_rng([20260905, k])`, conditional on the realised selection path. Per-year table rows 2022 (training only, reference) and each OOS segment: selected member, SR_train, SR_OOS, A0 SR, H\* SR, best-in-year SR, sign flag (negative years printed explicitly).

**§4.5 Families and seeds reported.** F1 s42: everything (both windows, per-year, top selections), F1 s2027 replicate: everything. F2–F5 s42: compact rows (N, N_eff, PBO, slope, P(loss), SEL SR, DSR at N_eff, SR_nested, haircut) on W_FULL and FROZEN. W_ALPHA: F1 s42 compact row.

**§4.7 Reading rules (AMENDMENT 5, verbatim meaning).** PBO ≥ 0.5 ⇒ 「按回测挑选在该家族上没有样本外价值」. DSR numbers are probabilities, not pass/fail on real data. T6 measures a defect of the research method; it is not an explanation of live results.
