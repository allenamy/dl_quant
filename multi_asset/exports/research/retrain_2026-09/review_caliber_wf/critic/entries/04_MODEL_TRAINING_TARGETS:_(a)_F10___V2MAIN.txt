#### definition
Let r5[t,s] = cache channel 0 = float16(clip(c_t/c_{t-5m} - 1, -0.3, 0.3)) (simple 5-minute return; VERIFIED bitwise vs raw kline zips, log-return rejected). Let E = cache row whose bar CLOSES at anchor N (ts%14400==0). Missing bars count as 0.

(a) F10 / V2MAIN DL target = y4s[E,s] = expm1( sum_{k=1..48} log1p(r5[E+k,s]) ) = PROD_{k=1..48}(1 + r5[E+k,s]) - 1, over rows E+1..E+48 = holding window (N, N+4h]; NaN if fewer than 46 finite bars. Stored float32 in /workspace/dlw_ext/data/dlw_targets.npz key y4s (sha 31d043e8...). It enters the loss LINEARLY as a book P&L: net_i = 1e4 * sum_s(w_i,s * y4s[i,s]) - COST*|dw|, loss = -mean(net) + LDD*ES5(net). No rank/label transform of y4s; YRZ/YR4s/y4old in the same npz are NOT read by any F10 training script. Caliber: compound simple ("true" simple 4h return), NOT sum-of-5m and NOT log.

(b) King LGBM label = within-anchor cross-sectional rank of y4: rr = rankdata(y4[i,m][ok]) / max(n-1,1) - 0.5, with y4[E,s] = sum_{k=0..47} r5[E+k,s] (rows E..E+47 = (N-5m, N+3h55m], i.e. includes the already-closed bar at N and excludes the 48th future bar), NaN if <46 finite bars, from /workspace/data/wide_fea_v2ext_meta.npz key y4; LGBMRegressor.fit(X, rr). Caliber of the underlying quantity: sum of simple 5m returns (bitwise identical to dlw y4old on all 10176 common anchors). The label is a rank, so it is invariant to any strictly increasing elementwise transform g(y4) (expm1, log1p, scaling) - the sum-vs-log-vs-expm1 question is IRRELEVANT for the king label at that level. It is NOT exactly invariant to replacing sum r5 by prod(1+r5)-1 (path-dependent cross terms): same-window Spearman(SUM, PROD) median 0.99978 (min 0.9929 over 40 anchors), median |SUM-PROD| 1.1 bps, ~49% of members shift rank by adjacent swaps (max |delta label| 0.045 on a [-0.5,0.5] scale) - practically negligible. The one-bar WINDOW shift ([E,E+47] vs [E+1,E+48]) is the dominant difference vs the F10 target (Spearman 0.968, median |y4s - y4old| 18.8 bps).

#### caveats
