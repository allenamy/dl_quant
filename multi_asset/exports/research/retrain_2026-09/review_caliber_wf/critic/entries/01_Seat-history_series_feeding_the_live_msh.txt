#### definition
Per name and 5-min bar closing at t: r5[t] = float16(clip(close[t]/close[t-5m] - 1, -0.3, 0.3)) (simple close-to-close return; NaN if bar missing). For a 4h anchor E and a leg L in {king, rev24, fund}: score s over members m (king = slow_pred_pinned[i, m]; rev24 = -f_rev_24h; fund = f_fund_ema_v1), z = nan_to_num(rankdata(s)/(n-1) - 0.5), z set to 0 where y4 is non-finite, z -= mean(z[finite y4]), z /= sum|z|; LR_L[E] = 1e4 * sum(z * nan_to_num(y4)) — i.e. bps per 4h of a unit-gross rank book. The y4 vector is a plain SUM of 48 float16 simple 5-min returns (float64 accumulation, cast float32, NaN if <46 finite bars) — no expm1/log1p/compounding anywhere. Two window conventions coexist in the series: (a) POD-BUILT rows (v3 bundle and the old 08-16 bundle, which supplies 790 of the current 900-row window): y4_pod[E] = sum_{k=0..47} r5[E + 5m*k] = bars closing in [E, E+3h55m] (includes the bar closing AT the anchor, excludes the bar closing at E+4h); rev24 score uses f_rev_24h = sum_{k=1..288} r5[E - 5m*k] (bars closing in [E-24h+5m... E-5m], excludes the anchor bar). (b) PRODUCER-APPENDED rows: y4_prod[E] = sum_{k=1..48} r5[E + 5m*k] = bars closing in (E, E+4h] from state/rolling.npz (NaN for names outside the 450-name data universe), rev24 score = sum_{k=0..287} r5[E - 5m*k] (includes the anchor bar), fund rank over the M1 base (xz_in_base). Same caliber (sum of simple 5m), one-bar (5-minute) window offset between (a) and (b).
