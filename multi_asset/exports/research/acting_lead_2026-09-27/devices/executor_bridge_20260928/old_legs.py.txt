def reshape_after_withhold(w, sizing_gross, redemean=True, rescale=True,
                           floors_usdt=None, strict=False):
    """Restore the two shaping properties a withhold destroys. Returns {"w", ...report...}.

    ★★★ THE DEFECT (measured, first live anchor 2026-08-01 12:00Z). The book is demeaned over all
        110 members, then the universe gate WITHHOLDS untradable names — PORTALUSDT at w = -1.80%.
        Nothing re-shapes the remainder, and the precise statement is what explains why nobody saw
        it: **the pop triggers a RE-SUMMATION OF GROSS but not a RE-DEMEANING.** `target_gross`
        becomes the sum of the surviving |notional|, so `w = notional/target_gross` makes sum|w|
        exactly 1.000000 while sum(w) keeps +1.797%. Of the two properties, unit-gross self-heals
        and market-neutrality is silently dropped — **and the gross half looking correct is the
        camouflage**.
        Two consequences, both equal to |w_popped| x sizing_gross: the book is SMALLER than
        decided (4194.26 against 4269.63) and NET LONG by 75.37 before a single order is sent.
        Unbounded: mainnet reports 169 zero-cap names against testnet's 50.

    ★ TWO SWITCHES, TWO JUSTIFICATIONS, SEPARATELY REVERTIBLE (lead's ruling):
        redemean — neutrality is the foundation of the risk argument. Order-preserving (a uniform
                   shift and an L1 rescale both preserve rank), so no alpha is given up.
                   ★★★ CORRECTION (2026-09-16, independent review REVIEW_fixprogram_progress_
                   2026-09-14 §7.1; the sentence above is kept VERBATIM and marked false here,
                   per FIXPROGRAM §11). "so no alpha is given up" DOES NOT FOLLOW. Rank
                   preservation is not alpha preservation: the book's P&L is a function of the
                   VALUES and SIGNS of the weights, not of their order. The reviewer re-ran this
                   very function and got
                       [+0.40, -0.59, -0.01]  ->  [+0.44586, -0.50, +0.05414]
                   — the ranking is preserved and the small SHORT becomes a LONG. Measured at
                   book scale (EXE-03 / PROD-25): of 109 combo anchors, 99 flip the direction of
                   a small position once names are withheld, and on 09-13 12Z 91.363% of the
                   remaining net short came from the producer's own net short rather than from
                   the withheld names. What survives of the original claim: the transform is
                   order-preserving. What does not: the inference from that to "no alpha lost".
                   Also unproven either way: producer and executor hold DIFFERENT intents after
                   reshaping, and a two-sided-scaling alternative has not been measured.
        rescale  — leverage is a RISK DECISION and must not be a by-product of tradability.
      They are approved together and must stay independently revertible; welded into one function
      neither could be rolled back alone.

    ★★ BUT `rescale` WITHOUT `redemean` IS REFUSED UNDER `strict`. Scaling a still-tilted book UP
       grows the net in proportion — strictly worse than doing nothing, not merely "less
       complete". A configuration that can express it will eventually be set that way by someone
       reverting the other half. Independence is about ROLLBACK; this is about a state that must
       not run.

    ★ IT REPORTS WHAT IT DID. A silent correction and a correction that did not happen are the
      same output — the shape that cost this repo four defects on 2026-08-01. `net_before` is the
      DEFECT'S SIZE, so the log answers "how much was wrong", not only "it is fixed now".

    ★ FLOOR INTERACTION: re-demeaning moves every weight, so a name can cross its min-notional
      floor. That is REPORTED and the function STOPS — one pass, never a silent
      demean/pop/demean loop, which would converge on a book nobody specified.
    """
    w = np.asarray(w, dtype=float).copy()
    g = float(sizing_gross)
    if strict and rescale and not redemean:
        raise ValueError(
            "rescale without redemean amplifies a tilted book: the net grows in proportion, "
            "which is strictly worse than leaving it alone. Enable redemean, or disable both.")

    gross_before = float(np.abs(w).sum()) * g
    net_before = float(w.sum()) * g
    before = w.copy()

    def _above_floor(vec):
        if not floors_usdt:
            return set()
        return {i for i in range(len(vec))
                if abs(float(vec[i])) * g >= float(floors_usdt.get(i, 0.0))}

    floor_before = _above_floor(w)
    if redemean:
        w = w - w.mean()
    if rescale:
        s = float(np.abs(w).sum())
        if s > 0:
            w = w / s                       # unit gross; the caller multiplies by sizing_gross
    floor_after = _above_floor(w)
    crossed = sorted(floor_before.symmetric_difference(floor_after))

    return {"w": w,
            "net_before": net_before, "net_after": float(w.sum()) * g,
            "gross_before": gross_before, "gross_after": float(np.abs(w).sum()) * g,
            "max_name_delta_pp": float(np.abs(w - before).max()) * 100.0,
            "floor_set_changed": bool(crossed),
            "names_crossed_floor": crossed,
            "iterations": 1,
            "redemean_applied": bool(redemean), "rescale_applied": bool(rescale),
            "caliber": ("net/gross are in USDT at `sizing_gross`; max_name_delta_pp is in "
                        "percentage POINTS of weight. One pass only — a crossed floor is "
                        "reported, never iterated away.")}
